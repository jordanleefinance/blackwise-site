AI on your terms

You control the AI. Not the other way around.

Every AI feature in BlackWise is built on these four rules. They apply to your personal workspace and your business workspace separately.

Off by default

Nothing happens until you say so

AI stays off until you turn it on, one workspace at a time. Switch it off whenever you like and every budget, dashboard and tool keeps working exactly the same.

Private to you

Your data answers your questions only

When AI is on, it only sees your own workspace to answer your own question. Nothing is pooled with other users, and nothing carries over from one session to the next.

Never used for training

Your finances don't teach anyone's model

We only work with AI providers whose terms forbid training on customer data. Your bank, loan and QuickBooks information is never used to build or improve an AI model.

Fully visible

See exactly what the AI saw

Every AI answer comes with a "See what AI saw" view, and each workspace keeps a log of every AI request so owners can review it at any time. No black boxes.

Families

Share money with someone, on your terms.

Plan with a partner, roommate or anyone you share bills with. You decide what they see, one account at a time.

How does family sharing work?

Open Family from your Personal dashboard and invite someone by email. They join with the code you send them, signed in with that email. Once they're in, you share "Our budget", a set of monthly limits for what you spend together, and your dashboard gets a Personal / Family switch. You can be in one family at a time and leave whenever you like.

What stays private?
  • Every account starts private. Nothing is shared until you choose.
  • Your personal budget doesn't change. "Our budget" counts only the accounts you chose to share.
  • Business accounts are never shared through a family.
  • If you give an accountant or advisor access, they never see another family member's accounts through you.
Can I share only part of an account?

Yes. For each account you pick Private, Balance only (they see the balance but not what you spend) or Shared (they see the balance and every transaction). For a bank account you share in full or as balance only, you can also count all of it, a percentage, or a set dollar amount each month toward the family. Your own Cash on hand then shows only your part. You can also mark a single transaction as family or personal.

What can my family see on a shared account?

The balance and its transactions, including who spent each one. Either of you can change a shared transaction's category, and those changes are kept in your activity history.

How do shared goals work?

Create a family goal, like a trip or a down payment, and pick the shared accounts that fund it. The family's part of each account counts toward the goal, and you can add money saved some other way. You'll both see the accounts and recent transactions behind it.

How does "Who owes who" work?

Record a dinner you covered, rent you split or money you collected for someone, and split a bill evenly across several people. It works with anyone, in your family or not. They see it in BlackWise when they sign in with that email, or once they sign up, and either of you can mark it paid. They see the amount and what it was for, never your bank details. No money moves through BlackWise: pay each other the way you already do.

What happens if someone leaves?

When you or another member leaves or is removed, access ends right away. Your accounts stop showing in the family, and your personal dashboard and budget stay exactly as they were.

About

Making complex money simple.

BlackWise is built by JMM Group, a finance, accounting and tax firm that works with individuals, nonprofits and growing businesses. We kept seeing the same thing: people make big money decisions without seeing the math behind them.

So we built one place where your personal and business finances live, connected to the professionals who help you, and able to answer real questions with your own data. Every number shows its formula. Nothing is a black box.

What we believe

  • Transparency first. You should see what you pay in interest, taxes and fees.
  • Education and tools belong together.
  • AI should help, never decide, and it should always have an off switch.
