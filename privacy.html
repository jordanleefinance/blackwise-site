1. Who we are

BlackWise ("BlackWise," "we," "us") is a personal and business finance app operated by JMM Group. This policy covers the BlackWise mobile app, the website at blackwiseai.com, and our related services (together, the "Service").

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and login credentials.
  • Information you enter: budgets, goals, scenarios, business details, notes and any documents you upload.
  • Messages: what you send us when you contact support or join the waitlist.

Information from accounts you connect

If you choose to connect a bank, card, loan, investment or accounting account, we receive financial information from that account. Section 3 explains exactly what we receive.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and error data: app version, device type, operating system and error logs. We use it to keep the Service working and to fix problems. We do not use analytics tools that track the screens or features you use.
  • Cookies and local storage: used to keep you signed in and to remember settings. We do not use advertising cookies.

3. Connected accounts: Plaid and QuickBooks

Bank, card, loan and investment accounts (Plaid)

We use Plaid Inc. ("Plaid") to connect your financial accounts. When you connect an account, you sign in through Plaid's secure screen. We never see or store your bank username or password. Depending on the features you use, Plaid may share the following with us:

  • Accounts and balances: account names, types, the last few digits of account numbers, and current and available balances.
  • Transactions: dates, amounts, merchant names, categories and descriptions.
  • Liabilities: credit card, student loan and mortgage details such as balances, interest rates, minimum payments and due dates.
  • Investments: holdings, securities, quantities, values and investment transactions.

By connecting an account you also agree to Plaid's handling of your data under the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. You can see and manage the connections you have shared through Plaid at my.plaid.com.

QuickBooks Online (Intuit)

Business users can connect a QuickBooks Online company. You approve the connection on Intuit's own screen. We never see your Intuit password. Intuit gives us an access token that lets BlackWise read:

  • Company information, such as the company name.
  • Financial reports: Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet and Statement of Cash Flows.

BlackWise does not currently create, change or delete anything in your QuickBooks company. If we add features that write to QuickBooks, such as letting an invited accountant post entries, we will update this policy first and those features will only work with your permission.

You can disconnect QuickBooks at any time in BlackWise, or from your QuickBooks account under Apps. When you disconnect, we revoke our access with Intuit and delete the stored tokens right away.

How we protect connection tokens

Access tokens from Plaid and Intuit are encrypted before they are stored, and only our servers can use them. They are never sent to your phone or browser and are never visible to other users.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: show your dashboards, run budgets and scenarios, and track goals.
  • To let people you invite, such as an accountant or advisor, see your workspace.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent fraud and fix errors.
  • To answer your questions and send account notices, such as security alerts or changes to these policies.
  • To improve the Service using aggregated or de-identified information that does not identify you.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your financial data for advertising. We do not use data from Plaid or QuickBooks for any purpose other than providing the features you use.

5. AI features

Some features use artificial intelligence to explain your numbers or suggest next steps. AI features are optional, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings. When they are on, we send only the information needed for your request, such as summarized totals, to our AI provider so it can generate your answer. We only use AI providers whose terms do not allow them to train their models on the data we send. AI output is educational and can be wrong, so check important decisions with a qualified professional.

6. How we share information

We share information only in these cases:

  • People you invite. If you invite an accountant, advisor or team member to a workspace, they can see that workspace's data. You can remove them at any time.
  • Service providers. Companies that help us run the Service, under contracts that limit how they use your data. These include our hosting provider (Vercel), our database and sign-in provider (Supabase), Plaid, Intuit, our AI provider (only when AI is on) and our email provider.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or BlackWise.
  • Business transfers. If BlackWise is part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may transfer, and this policy will continue to protect it.
  • With your consent. In any other case, only when you tell us to.

7. How long we keep information

  • We keep your account information while your account is open.
  • When you disconnect an account, we stop accessing it and delete its access tokens right away. You can ask us to delete data we previously pulled from that account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your personal and financial data within 30 days. We may keep limited records longer when the law requires it, such as billing records, or in backups that are overwritten on a regular schedule.

8. Security

We encrypt data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest, encrypt connection tokens separately, restrict access with database security rules so users can only see their own workspaces, and limit administrative access to authorized staff using multi-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will notify you as required by law.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect any linked account at any time.
  • Turn AI off in Settings.
  • Access, correct, export or delete your information by emailing info@blackwiseai.com. We will respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first.
  • Email choices: unsubscribe from marketing email using the link in any message. We will still send required account notices.

Depending on where you live, including California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, you may have additional rights to know, delete, correct or limit the use of your personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. We honor those requests at the email above. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. Age requirement

BlackWise is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. If we make a material change, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
