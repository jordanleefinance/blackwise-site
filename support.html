Support

We answer every message, usually within two business days.

Need help? Email info@blackwiseai.com. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. Please never send passwords, full account numbers or authenticator codes.

Connecting a bank

Go to Settings, then Connections, and choose Connect a bank. You sign in on Plaid's secure screen, and BlackWise never sees your bank password. Connecting a bank needs two-step verification, so the app asks you to set up an authenticator app first if you haven't.

Disconnecting an account

Go to Settings, then Connections, and choose Disconnect next to the account. We stop pulling data right away and delete the access token. You can also remove BlackWise at my.plaid.com.

Turning AI off

AI features are optional. Turn them off for any workspace in Settings. When AI is off, nothing from that workspace is sent to our AI provider.

Household sharing

Open Household from your Personal dashboard to invite someone, choose who sees each account, or leave. Answers to common questions, like what stays private and how partial sharing works, are on our household FAQ.

Lost your authenticator

Email us from the address on your account. We verify your identity before we reset two-step verification.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings and choose Delete account. This removes your workspaces, connections and data. We finish deleting within 30 days, as our Privacy Policy explains. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
