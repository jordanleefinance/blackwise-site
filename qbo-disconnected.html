QuickBooks is disconnected

Your QuickBooks Online company is no longer connected to BlackWise. Here's what that means:

  • BlackWise can no longer read any data from your QuickBooks company.
  • Nothing in your QuickBooks company was changed or deleted.
  • Your business dashboard in BlackWise will stop updating.

Want to reconnect?

Open the BlackWise app, go to your business workspace, and tap Connect QuickBooks. You'll sign in on Intuit's screen and approve access again.

Want your data deleted?

Email info@blackwiseai.com and we'll delete the QuickBooks data BlackWise saved for your workspace. See our Privacy Policy for details.
